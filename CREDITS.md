# Credits

**Squad Six was created by arctco.** Everything up to and including version 1.1.4 is their work:
the UE4SS runtime, the six-slot squad-selection and lineup UI, the deployment-placement logic, and
the cooked mission overrides. That includes the manual testing and the hand-picked list of
missions that raise to six, or deliberately stay at four, which is the part arctco asked to be
credited for.

- Original page: [Nexus Mods, Star Wars: Zero Company mod 44](https://www.nexusmods.com/starwarszerocompany/mods/44)
- Final upstream release: 1.1.4 (2026-09-03), imported unmodified in commit `upstream-1.1.4`

arctco discontinued the mod and gave permission for others to continue it:

> If you want to experiment with it or improve it yourself, go ahead, I'd appreciate credit for
> the manual work and hand picked missions that went into it.

Players who reported the bugs that shaped the mission guards (named in [docs/runtime-notes.md](docs/runtime-notes.md)): MortisVenunum,
Tesel, FoxFlight.
