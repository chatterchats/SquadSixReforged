# Credits

**Squad Six Reforged continues Squad Six, which was created by arctco.** Everything up to and
including Squad Six 1.1.4 is their work: the UE4SS runtime, the six-slot squad-selection and
lineup UI, the deployment-placement logic, and the cooked mission overrides. That includes the manual testing and the hand-picked list of
missions that raise to six, or deliberately stay at four, which is the part arctco asked to be
credited for.

- Original page: [Nexus Mods, Star Wars: Zero Company mod 44](https://www.nexusmods.com/starwarszerocompany/mods/44)
- Final upstream release: 1.1.4 (2026-09-03), imported unmodified in commit `upstream-1.1.4`

arctco discontinued the mod and gave permission for others to continue it:

> If you want to experiment with it or improve it yourself, go ahead, I'd appreciate credit for
> the manual work and hand picked missions that went into it.

The archived v1 runtime retains arctco's code under that permission. V2 reimplements the gameplay
logic as generated Blueprints and preserves the established mission choices and placement work.
No licence has been chosen yet.

Players who reported the bugs that shaped the mission guards (named in
[docs/runtime-notes.md](docs/runtime-notes.md)): MortisVenunum, Tesel, FoxFlight.
